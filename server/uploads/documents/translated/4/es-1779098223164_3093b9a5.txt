INDIVIDUAL INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

THIS INDIVIDUAL INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is executed and entered into by and between the “Company” (or “Client”) and the “Contractor” as identified by their respective registered account information on the [Platform Name] website, effective as of the date on which this Agreement is electronically accepted by both parties on the platform.

The legal names, mailing addresses, and contact information of both parties shall be as provided in their official [Platform Name] account registration records and shall be deemed incorporated herein by reference..

BACKGROUND

Company desires to engage Contractor to perform certain services for Company as an independent contractor. Company and Contractor intend this Agreement to govern each of their rights and obligations with respect to such engagement.

AGREEMENT

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained herein, and intending to be legally bound hereby, the parties agree as follows:

1. Engagement; Scope of Services.

Subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement, Company hereby engages Contractor to perform the services for Company as described in the individual task postings assigned and accepted by the Contractor via the [Platform Name] website. Each accepted task posting constitutes a supplement to and forms part of this Agreement as to the scope, deliverables, schedule, and any other relevant details for that particular assignment.

"Task Posting" refers to the specific assignment details published by the Company (or on its behalf) on the [Platform Name] website and accepted by the Contractor, including but not limited to service description, deliverables, schedule, compensation, and any special terms, all of which are deemed incorporated into this Agreement.

2. Compensation.

Company shall compensate Contractor for Services performed in accordance with the compensation amount, rate, payment method, and payment schedule as specified in the relevant task posting accepted by the Contractor via the [Platform Name] website.

All matters relating to payment, disputes, or reimbursement shall be governed by the terms and records contained in each task posting and the official records of the [Platform Name] platform.

Unless otherwise stated in the assignment posting, no additional payment requests or expense reimbursements shall be accepted.

If the Contractor has any questions, disputes, or disagreements regarding compensation or reimbursement for a specific assignment, the Contractor shall promptly notify the Company (Client).  Pending resolution of the dispute, the Contractor may temporarily suspend the disputed services until the matter is resolved and both parties confirm next steps.

3. Term. This Agreement shall remain in effect for as long as the Contractor continues to accept and complete assignments issued via the [Platform Name] website, unless terminated earlier pursuant to the termination provisions herein.

4. Independent Contractor.

(A). Contractor is an independent contractor and shall not be deemed an employee of Company. Both parties agree that the Company shall not control or direct the manner or means by which contractor performs the Services, including but not limited to, the time and place Contractor performs the Services; the Services performed are outside the usual course of the Company's business; and Contractor is customarily engaged in an independently established trade, occupation, or business of the same nature as the Services performed.

(B). Unless otherwise stated in this Agreement, Contractor shall furnish, at Contractor’s own expense, the materials, equipment, supplies, and other resources necessary to perform the Services. The Company shall provide with access to its premises, materials, information, and systems to the extent necessary for the performance of the Services.

(C). This Agreement shall not be construed to create any association, partnership, joint venture, employment, or agency relationship between Contractor and the Company for any purpose. Contractor has no authority (and shall not hold itself out as having authority) to bind the Company and Contractor shall not make any agreements or representations on the Company's behalf without the Company's prior written consent.

(D). Contractor will not be eligible to participate in any vacation, group medical or life insurance, disability, profit sharing or retirement benefits, or any other fringe benefits or benefit plans offered by the Company to its employees, and the Company will not be responsible for withholding or paying any income, payroll, social security, or other federal, state, or local taxes, making any insurance contributions, including for unemployment or disability, or obtaining workers’ compensation insurance on Contractor’s behalf. Contractor shall be responsible for, and shall indemnify the Company against, all such taxes or contributions, including penalties and interest. Any persons employed or engaged by Contractor in connection with the performance of the Services shall be Contractor’s employees or contractors, and Contractor shall be fully responsible for them and fully indemnify the Company against any claims made by or on behalf of any such employee or contractor.

(E). Neither party shall be responsible for the acts or omissions of the other party hereto nor the acts or omissions of the employees of the other party hereto, except for acts or omissions of the other party or such other party’s employees caused by the grossly negligent or willful acts or omissions of such party. Neither party shall have the authority to speak for, represent or obligate the other party hereto in any way without either the express prior written consent by the other party.
